# Executive Summary

## Project
**Slyva Crescent Hotel Booking Analysis**

---

## Overview

This analysis examined **119,390 hotel booking records** from the **City Hotel** and **Resort Hotel**, covering the period **1 July 2015 to 31 August 2017**.

The objective was to help management better understand guest behaviour by answering key business questions, including:

- Where guests come from
- How much they spend
- How long they stay
- Which guests are most likely to cancel their bookings

The findings provide actionable insights to support operational improvements, revenue optimization, and strategic decision-making.

---

# Key Findings

## Guest Origin

Portugal is the hotel's largest source market with **48,590 bookings**, followed by:

- United Kingdom
- France
- Spain
- Germany

These markets represent the hotel's strongest customer base and provide opportunities for targeted marketing and customer retention initiatives.

---

## Guest Spending

The **Average Daily Rate (ADR)** across all bookings is **101.83**, representing the average revenue earned per occupied room per day.

The highest ADR recorded was **5,400.00**. Although this value is considerably higher than the average, it was retained because there was no evidence suggesting it was a data quality issue.

---

## Length of Stay

Guests stayed an average of **3.43 nights**, indicating that the majority of bookings were for short-term stays.

This insight can inform pricing strategies, weekend promotions, and extended-stay offers.

---

## Booking Cancellations

The analysis identified a notable difference in cancellation behaviour between the two hotel types.

| Hotel | Cancellation Rate |
|--------|------------------:|
| City Hotel | **41.73%** |
| Resort Hotel | **27.76%** |

The higher cancellation rate at City Hotel suggests an opportunity to review booking policies, confirmation processes, and customer engagement strategies.

---

## Additional Insights

- **August** recorded the highest number of bookings, making it the busiest period during the analysis.
- **Transient** customers represented the largest customer segment.
- The largest family booking included **10 children**, indicating occasional demand for family-focused accommodation and services.

---

# Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. Strengthen marketing efforts in Portugal and other high-performing international markets.
2. Review cancellation policies and booking confirmation processes at the City Hotel to reduce cancellations.
3. Increase staffing levels and operational readiness during peak periods, particularly in August.
4. Introduce loyalty programmes and targeted offers for Transient customers to improve customer retention.
5. Expand family-friendly services and accommodation options to better serve larger family groups.

---

# Conclusion

The analysis provides valuable insights into guest demographics, spending patterns, stay duration, and cancellation behaviour.

These findings equip hotel management with evidence-based recommendations to improve operational planning, enhance the guest experience, reduce revenue leakage, and maximize revenue across both hotel properties.
