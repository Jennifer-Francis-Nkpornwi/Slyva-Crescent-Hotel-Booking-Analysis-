# Key Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. **Strengthen marketing efforts** in Portugal, the United Kingdom, France, Spain, and Germany to sustain and grow bookings from the hotel's strongest source markets.

2. **Reduce cancellations at City Hotel** by reviewing cancellation policies, introducing flexible booking options, and implementing reminder communications before arrival.

3. **Prepare for peak demand in August** by increasing staffing levels, optimizing room allocation, and ensuring operational readiness during high-demand periods.

4. **Invest in customer retention strategies** for transient guests through loyalty programs, personalized offers, and targeted marketing campaigns.

5. **Expand family-oriented services** by offering larger room options, children's amenities, and family travel packages to better accommodate guests travelling with children.
