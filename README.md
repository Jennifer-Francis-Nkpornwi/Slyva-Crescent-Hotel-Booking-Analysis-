## Project Overview

This project is an **end-to-end Power BI analysis** of a real-world hotel booking dataset containing **119,390 booking records** from a **City Hotel** and a **Resort Hotel** between **July 2015 and August 2017**.

The project began as a business intelligence exercise to answer stakeholder questions around guest behavior, booking trends, spending, length of stay, and cancellations. It later evolved into a **Commercial Impact Analysis** for me, demonstrating how the same dataset can uncover deeper business value when different business questions are asked.

---

## Business Objective

The hotel management wanted answers to key business questions:

- Which country do most travelers come from? List the 5 countries with the most passengers
- Who has the most ADR? How much?
- Specify the mean of the total ADR? (two decimal places)
- Specify the average number of nights spent. (two decimal places)
- Who has booked the hotel with the most children and babies? 
- How long do they stay?
- Who cancels their bookings?
- And any other 3 business case questions of your own. 

Beyond answering these questions, I expanded the analysis to identify the **commercial impact of cancellations**, quantify revenue at risk, and provide actionable recommendations for business decision-makers.

## Dashboard 2 – Commercial Impact

Following valuable stakeholder feedback, I revisited the same dataset with a different business question:

> **What is the financial impact of booking cancellations?**

Using **Average Daily Rate (ADR)** and **Length of Stay** as a proxy for booking value, I developed a second dashboard focused on commercial performance and decision-making.

### Dashboard KPIs

- 💰 Estimated Booking Revenue

# Key Business Insights

- Approximately **$16.73M** in estimated booking value was associated with cancelled reservations.
- **City Hotel** accounted for the highest revenue at risk from cancellations.
- **Portugal** was the strongest source market for hotel bookings.
- **Transient** customers contributed the largest share of booking value.
- Short average stays present opportunities to increase revenue through longer-stay offers.

---

# Stakeholder Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I recommended that management:

- Review City Hotel's cancellation policies to reduce revenue leakage.
- Strengthen retention strategies for high-value customer segments.
- Increase marketing investment in high-performing source markets.
- Encourage longer stays through targeted promotions.
- Monitor **Revenue at Risk** alongside cancellation rates to support commercial decision-making.

# Key Learning

One of the biggest lessons from this project was:

> **The same data can tell completely different stories—it all depends on the questions you ask.**

The first dashboard answered the questions in the project brief.

The second dashboard answered a different question:

> **"What decision should the business make next?"**

That shift transformed the project from descriptive reporting into commercial insight, reinforcing that great analytics should not only explain **what happened**, but also guide **what should happen next**.
