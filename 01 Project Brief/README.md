# Project Brief

## Hotel Booking Performance Analysis

### Background

Slyva Crescent Hotel Group operates both **City Hotel** and **Resort Hotel** properties, serving guests from diverse markets and customer segments. While booking data is collected daily, management lacks a clear understanding of guest behavior, booking trends, and cancellation patterns needed to support strategic business decisions.

The Operations Management Team has commissioned an analysis of historical booking data covering **1 July 2015 to 31 August 2017**. The dataset contains **119,390 booking records**, including both completed and cancelled reservations.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze historical booking data and provide actionable insights that help management:

- Understand guest booking behavior.
- Evaluate hotel performance.
- Identify booking and cancellation trends.
- Support operational and revenue-focused decision-making.

---

## Scope

The analysis will focus on:

- Guest origin and booking distribution.
- Average Daily Rate (ADR).
- Length of stay.
- Customer types.
- Booking cancellations.
- Additional insights that support business decision-making.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis should answer the following questions:

- Which country do most travelers come from?
- What are the top five countries by bookings?
- Which guest recorded the highest ADR, and what was the value?
- What is the average ADR?
- What is the average number of nights stayed?
- Which booking included the highest number of children and babies?
- What additional business insights can be identified from the data?

---

## Expected Deliverables

- Cleaned and validated dataset.
- Interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Executive summary of findings.
- Business questions with supporting insights and recommendations.

---

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it:

- Produces accurate and reliable analysis.
- Clearly communicates booking and guest behavior trends.
- Identifies actionable insights from historical booking data.
- Supports management with evidence-based recommendations.

---

# Project Evolution

After completing the original analysis, I received stakeholder feedback encouraging me to **move beyond descriptive reporting and focus on commercial impact**.

Rather than treating the project as complete, I revisited the same dataset with a new business question:

> **"What is the financial impact of booking cancellations?"**

This led to the development of a second **Commercial Impact Dashboard**, where I:

- Estimated total booking revenue using ADR and length of stay.
- Quantified **$16.73M** in estimated revenue at risk from cancelled bookings.
- Identified customer segments contributing the most booking value and cancellation risk.
- Translated analytical findings into practical business recommendations for revenue protection and customer retention.

This evolution demonstrates an important lesson in analytics:

> **The same dataset can tell different stories—it all depends on the questions you ask.**

More importantly, it reflects my approach as an analyst: **delivering insights that help stakeholders make better business decisions, not just dashboards that report historical metrics.**
